$ mailcheck

Who can send email as you?

Type a domain to see who is allowed to send email as it, whether that's locked down, and the exact records to fix it. Free, open source, and it runs in your browser.

DKIM selector

DKIM keys can only be found by name. This page tries the common ones. If yours uses another, type it here.

What this page sees

The domains you check are sent to Cloudflare's public DNS service to look up their records. Nothing is sent to me, nothing is stored, and there are no cookies or analytics.
